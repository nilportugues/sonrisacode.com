Library

Every song you own, neatly sorted.

Sonrisa scans your phone and reads the real tags inside your files, so titles, artists and albums are right.

  • MP3, FLAC, M4A, OGG and more
  • Songs, albums, artists and playlists
  • Instant search and per-folder filters

Artwork

Covers and artist photos, found for you.

It uses the cover inside your file first. If there isn't one, it can look it up online. You can also pick an image yourself.

  • Embedded covers come first
  • Online lookup you can switch off
  • Fill in all missing images in one go
  • Fix a wrong cover or artist in two taps
Stats

See how you really listen.

Listening time, plays and a ranking of your top songs, plus a calendar heatmap. Tap any day to see what you played.

  • Rank by plays or by minutes
  • All time, last 30 days or last 7 days
  • Counts only real listening, not skips
  • Private: it never leaves your phone

FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Does it stream music?

No. Sonrisa plays the music files already on your phone. It doesn't need a subscription or a connection to play.

Which formats does it play?

Common audio formats such as MP3, FLAC, M4A and OGG. It reads the tags inside the files (ID3, Vorbis, MP4) for titles, artists and albums.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and your music is there. There's nothing to sign up for.

What leaves my phone?

Only the optional artwork and song-details lookup: the song title and artist name are sent to the Deezer and iTunes catalogs. You can switch it off in Settings. Your stats, playlists and listening history never leave your device.

Can I play music on my TV?

Yes. Use the Cast button to send music to a Chromecast on the same Wi‑Fi network.

Which languages are available?

72. The app uses your phone's language. You can also choose another one in the app's Settings, or on Android 13+ in the per-app language settings.
