Privacy Policy

Sonrisa Music Player (Android, package com.nilportugues.sonrisa_music_player)
Effective date: November 16, 2026

In short: Sonrisa plays the music that is already on your phone. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your music, playlists and listening statistics stay on your device. The only things that leave your phone are the optional online lookups for album art and song details described below, and you can turn them off.

1. Who we are

Sonrisa Music Player ("Sonrisa", "the app") is developed by an independent developer ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, contact us at CONTACT_EMAIL.

2. Data we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting services, and it does not use the Android advertising ID.
  • We do not run any server that receives data from the app. We never see your music, your playlists or how you listen.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone.

3. Data stored only on your device

To work, the app keeps the following on your phone, in its private storage:

  • An index of your audio files and their tags (title, artist, album and similar).
  • Your favorites, playlists, playback queue and position.
  • Listening statistics (what you played, when and for how long), used for the Stats screen and the yearly Recap.
  • Album art and artist photos, either taken from your files or downloaded, and your settings.

This data never leaves your device. You can clear the statistics and the downloaded images from the app's Settings, and uninstalling the app deletes all of it. Android may include app data in your personal device backup, as it does for other apps; that backup is controlled by your Google account settings, not by us.

4. Permissions

  • Music and audio / storage: to find and play the audio files on your device. The app does not read your photos or videos.
  • Changing audio files: only when you edit a song's tags or use "Fill in metadata from the internet". Android asks for your permission before any file is changed.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to show the playback controls in the notification and on the lock screen while music plays.
  • Internet, network and Wi‑Fi state: for the optional online lookups and to find Chromecast devices on your Wi‑Fi network.

5. Optional online lookups

When a song has no album art or is missing details such as the artist, album or year, the app can look them up in public music catalogs. Both features are on by default and can be turned off in Settings ("Album art from the internet" and "Fill in metadata from the internet").

  • What is sent: only the song title and artist name (or the cleaned-up file name), as a search query. No account, device identifier or listening history is sent.
  • Who receives it: the Deezer API (api.deezer.com, images from dzcdn.net) and the Apple iTunes Search API (itunes.apple.com).
  • Like any website, these services receive your IP address and the request itself, and they handle it under their own privacy policies: Deezer and Apple.
  • The results (images and tags) are saved on your device so the same song is not looked up again.

6. Choosing an image by hand

If you choose "Choose image…", the app opens Google Images inside the app with a search for the song or artist. That page is provided by Google, which receives the search and may set cookies under the Google Privacy Policy. The image you pick is saved on your device.

7. Chromecast

If you cast music, the app uses the Google Cast SDK, which is part of Google Play services, to find and control Chromecast devices on your network. The song is streamed directly from your phone to the Chromecast over your local Wi‑Fi network and is not uploaded to us. Google Play services processes data under the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

9. Security

Data stays in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Online lookups use encrypted connections (HTTPS).

10. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data on our side, there is nothing about you for us to access, correct or delete. You stay in control of the data on your device: clear it from Settings or uninstall the app. If you live in the EU/EEA, the UK or another region with data protection laws, you can contact us or your local data protection authority with any concern.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change ships.
