FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Does it upload my photos anywhere?

No. There's no cloud and no account. Your photos stay on your phone. When you cast, they go straight from your phone to the TV over your home Wi‑Fi.

Can I see WhatsApp images, downloads and screenshots?

Yes. By default you see your camera photos. Tap the title to switch to All photos. The app remembers your choice.

What do I need to watch on my TV?

A Chromecast or a TV with Chromecast built in, on the same Wi‑Fi as your phone. Tap the Cast button and choose your TV.

Will my videos play on an older Chromecast?

Yes. Videos an older Chromecast can't play (HEVC, 4K, HDR) are converted on your phone to 1080p before sending.

Can I delete photos by accident?

Android asks for its own confirmation before any photo is deleted.

Which languages are available?

English and Spanish.
