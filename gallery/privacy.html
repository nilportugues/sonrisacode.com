Privacy Policy

Sonrisa Gallery (Android, package com.nilportugues.sonrisa_gallery)
Effective date: November 16, 2026

In short: Sonrisa Gallery shows the photos and videos that are already on your phone. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing leaves your phone except the photos and videos you choose to send to a Chromecast, and those travel only over your local Wi‑Fi network.

1. Who we are

Sonrisa Gallery ("the app") is developed by an independent developer ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, contact us at CONTACT_EMAIL.

2. Data we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting services, and it does not use the Android advertising ID.
  • We do not run any server that receives data from the app. We never see your photos, your videos or how you use the app.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone.

3. Data stored only on your device

To work, the app keeps the following on your phone, in its private storage:

  • Your settings (theme, language, grouping and sort order, and whether to show only camera photos or all photos).
  • Temporary copies of videos converted so an older Chromecast can play them. These are a cache and Android may remove them at any time.

The app reads the date, size, resolution and folder of your photos and videos to sort and group them, and shows them to you on request; it does not copy or upload this information anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data. Android may include app data in your personal device backup, as it does for other apps; that backup is controlled by your Google account settings, not by us.

4. Permissions

  • Photos and videos / storage: to find and show the photos and videos on your device. On Android 14 and later you can grant access to only the items you select. The app does not read your music or audio files and does not use the microphone.
  • Deleting photos: only when you choose "Delete". On Android 11 and later, Android asks for your confirmation before anything is removed. On Android 10 and earlier, the app asks for storage permission to delete.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to show a notification while you are casting, so the slideshow keeps running with the screen off.
  • Internet, network and Wi‑Fi state, Wi‑Fi multicast: to find Chromecast devices on your Wi‑Fi network and send them the photos and videos you choose.
  • Wake lock and vibration: to keep casting while the screen is off, and for subtle touch feedback.

5. Chromecast

If you cast, the app uses the Google Cast SDK, which is part of Google Play services, to find and control Chromecast devices on your network. While you cast, the app runs a small web server on your phone that serves only the photos and videos you sent, each protected by a random token, to the Chromecast on your local network. Photos are resized and videos may be converted on your phone before sending. Nothing is uploaded to us or to any server on the internet by the app. Google Play services processes data under the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Sharing

When you tap "Share", the app hands the selected photos or videos to the Android share sheet, and you choose the app that receives them. What happens next is governed by that app's privacy policy.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

8. Security

Settings stay in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. While casting, only the items you sent can be requested from your phone, and only with their random token.

9. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data on our side, there is nothing about you for us to access, correct or delete. You stay in control of the data on your device: uninstall the app to remove all of it. If you live in the EU/EEA, the UK or another region with data protection laws, you can contact us or your local data protection authority with any concern.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change ships.
