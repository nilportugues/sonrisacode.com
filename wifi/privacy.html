Privacy Policy

Sonrisa Wi‑Fi (Android, package com.nilportugues.sonrisa_wifi)
Effective date: November 17, 2026

In short: Sonrisa Wi‑Fi reads the Wi‑Fi name and password from your router's sticker, on your phone. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you scan is not stored by the app and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

1. Who we are

Sonrisa Wi‑Fi ("the app") is developed by an independent developer ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, contact us at nilopc@gmail.com.

2. Data we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting services, and it does not use the Android advertising ID.
  • We do not run any server that receives data from the app. We never see the camera image, the photos you pick, or the network names and passwords the app reads.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone.

3. How the app handles what you scan

  • The camera image, or the photo you pick from your gallery, is processed on your phone to find a Wi‑Fi QR code or the printed network name and password. A photo taken with Read is kept only temporarily in the app's private cache, which other apps cannot read and Android may clear at any time. It is never uploaded.
  • The network name and password are shown to you so you can check and edit them. They are only kept by Android if you choose to save the network in Android's own "Save this network?" dialog.
  • The list of nearby networks is used on your phone only, to correct a misread network name. It is not stored or sent anywhere.

4. Permissions

  • Camera: to read the sticker on your router.
  • Precise and approximate location: only while reading text, to scan for nearby Wi‑Fi networks and correct a misread network name. Android requires this permission to scan for networks. The app does not read or record your position. If you deny it, the app still works, just without name correction.
  • Wi‑Fi state and change Wi‑Fi state: to see nearby networks and to offer the network to Android so it can ask you to save it.
  • Photos: when you tap Gallery, Android's photo picker lets you choose one photo. The app only receives the photo you pick and does not get access to the rest of your photos.
  • Vibration: declared by a standard component the app is built with. It gives no access to your data.
  • Internet: the app works offline and does not send what you scan anywhere. See section 5 about Google ML Kit.

5. Google ML Kit

QR codes and printed text are recognized with Google ML Kit, which runs on your phone. The images are processed on the device and are not sent to Google or to us. As described in Google's ML Kit terms, ML Kit may contact Google servers to receive updates and may send Google anonymous metrics about how the recognition performs. These metrics do not include the images or the text read.

6. Clipboard

When you tap Copy, or when Android does not let the app save the network, the app copies the password to your clipboard so you can paste it in Wi‑Fi settings. Other apps you use may be able to read the clipboard, as with anything you copy.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

8. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data on our side, there is nothing about you for us to access, correct or delete. Networks you save are managed in your phone's Wi‑Fi settings, where you can remove them at any time. If you live in the EU/EEA, the UK or another region with data protection laws, you can contact us or your local data protection authority with any concern.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change ships.
